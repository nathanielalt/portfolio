Project 01 · UTIAS Research

Aeroacoustics Parametric Research Project

Overview

Completed as part of my research internship at the University of Toronto Institute for Aerospace Studies (UTIAS). Due to their convenience and versatility, many aeroacoustics labs have replaced wall-mounted microphones in favour of remote microphone probes (RMPs) connected to the surface through a tube. Despite their improvements, RMPs require calibration through a signal transfer function, as the pressure waves are distorted after travelling through the tube. My project aims to determine the effect of various parameters on the resulting transfer function. Some of these parameters included: tube length, tube diameter, ambient temperature and even humidity. As part of this project, numerous side projects were made including a sensor box, a bayesian-inference MCMC algorithm to clean data, and various experiment apparatuses.
