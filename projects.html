Projects

Four builds, four years of figuring things out.

From a cardboard wind tunnel to a UTIAS research internship — each project page below has the full overview, tools, pictures, challenges, and results.

01 · UTIAS Research

Aeroacoustics Parametric Research Project

Completed as part of a research internship at UTIAS, determining the effect of tube length, diameter, temperature, and humidity on remote microphone probe (RMP) transfer functions.
02 · RSX Aerial Design

Cansat Autonomous Glider

An autonomous glider for the AAS CANSAT competition, launched 700m into the air to drop an egg payload within a designated zone. Focus: mechanical design of the body, nosecone, and tail.
03 · Science Fair

Flow Control Research Project

Investigating vortex generator placement and gap spacing on stall AOA mitigation for a NACA 2412 airfoil, tested in a homemade flow-visualization wind tunnel.

04 · Personal Project

Lattice-Boltzmann Method CFD

A simplified, cloud/web-based CFD tool built on the Lattice-Boltzmann Method, aimed at beginners, hobbyists, and design teams who don't need a cluttered, heavyweight solver UI.
